1. Problem Understanding

Domain: Contract / Statement of Work (SoW) auditing in Fixed Price projects.

Pain Point: Vague, incomplete, or non-standard language in SoWs allows scope creep.

Goal: Before the SoW goes out, your AI should detect:

Non-specific deliverables (e.g., “a few,” “some,” “# databases”)

Missing standard sections (Scope, Deliverables, Timeline, Acceptance Criteria, etc.)

Ambiguous formatting (tables, bulleted lists, missing numbering)

Spelling / grammar errors (hurt professionalism and clarity)

Inconsistent terminology

Lack of measurable quantities

2. High-Level Working Outline
Step A — Input & Parsing

Input types:

Word docs

PDFs

Plain text

Text extraction & structure recognition:

Use python-docx / PyPDF2 to extract text.

Use NLP chunking to split into sections (Scope, Deliverables, etc.).

Detect tables, bullet lists, numbering.

Step B — Standard Structure Check

Verify that the SoW includes the essential sections:

Scope of Work

Deliverables (with counts)

Timeline / Milestones

Acceptance Criteria

Assumptions & Dependencies

Pricing & Payment Terms

Change Control Clause

Output: A checklist highlighting missing sections.

Step C — Specificity & Quantification Check

Look for vague or non-quantified terms:

Keywords: few, some, etc., as needed, #, ~, approximately

Sentences without measurable units (e.g., “build databases” without a number)

Unclear nouns (“tables” — database tables or furniture?)

Output: Suggested rewording, e.g.:

Original: “Build # Redshift databases”
Suggested: “Build 3 AWS Redshift databases, each with X tables.”

Step D — Ambiguity & Risk Detection

Identify scope creep risk phrases:

“As per client requirements”

“If needed”

“Additional work may be done”

Check for range terms without upper bound:

“5–10 reports” → clarify which will be charged.

Step E — Formatting & Professionalism

Detect missing numbering in lists.

Flag inconsistent capitalization (e.g., “AWS redshift” vs “AWS Redshift”).

Correct typos & grammar.

Step F — Output & Action

Scorecard: Specificity score, structure completeness score, professionalism score.

Risk Summary: Top 5 ambiguous items.

Auto-Redline Option: AI generates revised SoW text with corrections in place.

3. Tech Stack Suggestion

Text Extraction: python-docx, PyPDF2, or textract
NLP Processing: spaCy (NER, POS tagging), regex for pattern matching
LLM Agent Layer:

Use OpenAI GPT-4 or Claude 3 as a SoW Risk Auditor with a structured prompt.

Chain of thought: Extract → Classify → Suggest Fix → Output in structured JSON.

Frontend:

Streamlit app for uploading SoW, viewing risk score & recommendations.

Optional:

Integrate with contract management systems via API.

4. Example Workflow

Upload: User uploads SoW.docx

AI Agent Reads & Splits into sections.

Checklist Validation:

Missing “Acceptance Criteria” → Flag it.

Risk Scan:

Found: “Make # Redshift databases” → Ask for specific number.

Report:

Specificity Score: 68/100

Risks:

Deliverable 2: vague count (“# databases”)

Deliverable 5: unclear scope (“as needed”)

Suggested Fixes:

Replace “# databases” with exact count: e.g., “Build 3 databases.”

Define exact conditions for “as needed” tasks.

5. Extra Smart Features

Template enforcement: AI auto-formats SoW into company-approved template.

Risk prediction: Using past projects, predict likelihood of scope creep from vague terms.

Client-friendly redlines: Suggest alternative text that is clear yet collaborative.
